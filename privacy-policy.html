Privacy Policy — brocode gamer

Last updated: 2026-09-12

This application is a personal-use tool built and operated solely by the developer of the brocode gamer content channel. It is used only to upload the developer's own videos to the developer's own YouTube channel.

The application does not collect, store, share, or process any personal data belonging to any other user.
